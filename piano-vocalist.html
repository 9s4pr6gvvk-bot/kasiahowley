01About Kasia

A piano vocalist for your day.

Kasia performs as a solo piano vocalist or alongside a full band — for wedding ceremonies, drinks receptions, corporate functions and private parties. She's performed as a soloist at venues including the Royal Albert Hall, Trafalgar Square and the London O2 Indigo Arena, bringing the same polish to a wedding breakfast as to a concert stage.

Every booking is personal. Kasia takes time to understand what matters to you — from aisle walk songs to first dance moments — and delivers a performance that feels exactly right for your day.

Every booking is bespoke — get in touch for a personalised quote and package tailored to your event.
